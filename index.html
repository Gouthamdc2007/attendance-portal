Key Features

Engineered for SSN Standards

Built with enterprise cloud readiness, precise Anna University regulation tracking, and responsive UX.

Real-Time Cloud Synchronization

Instant persistence for attendance logs, leave submissions, and administrative user assignments.

75% Minimum Compliance Radar

Calculates required classes needed to restore eligibility and warns students before condonation limits.

Leave & On-Duty (OD) Workflow

Students file medical or sports leaves with documentary justification; faculty reviews with one click.

CSV & PDF Report Exports

Generate Anna University compliance sheets, batch summaries, and monthly attendance logs instantly.

Targeted Circulars & Notices

Admin and department heads broadcast urgent exam notices and holiday schedules directly to student dashboards.

Strict Role Isolation

Students cannot alter logs; staff can only mark assigned classes; Admin retains full institutional override.

About the Institution

SSN College of Engineering, Chennai

Founded in 1996 by Dr. Shiv Nadar, Founder Chairman of HCL Technologies, SSN College of Engineering stands as a premier autonomous institution in Kalavakkam, Chennai. Accredited with NAAC A++ and NBA, SSN is renowned for world-class technical education, research endowments, and holistic student development.
